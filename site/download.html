Let's get Stashd on your Mac.

Five short steps, about a minute.

Your download should start in a moment.Download Stashd

Free. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 or later.

  1. 1

    Open the download

    Find Stashd.dmg in your Downloads folder, or click it at the bottom of your browser, and double-click it.

    Downloads
    DMGStashd.dmgjust now
  2. 2

    Drag Stashd into Applications

    A small window opens. Drag the Stashd icon onto the Applications folder. If it asks, click Replace.

    Stashd
    →
    Applications
  3. 3

    Open it the first time

    Open Stashd from Applications. Your Mac will say it can't check it, because Stashd isn't from the App Store yet. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

    Privacy & Security
    "Stashd" was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  4. 4

    Click OK when your Mac asks

    The first time you save, your Mac asks if Stashd can see your browser's tabs. Click OK. It asks once for each app, like Chrome or Safari.

    To reopen the exact Claude or ChatGPT chat, also turn on Stashd in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. This one is optional.

    "Stashd" wants to control "Google Chrome".This lets Stashd see which tabs are open, so it can save them.
    Don't AllowOK
  5. 5

    Press ⌃S

    That's it. Stashd lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen, as a little folder. Next time you're in the middle of something, press Control-S.

Have an Intel Mac?

The download is for Apple silicon. On an Intel Mac, paste this into Claude Code and it will build Stashd for you:

Download and install the Stashd app from https://github.com/aryangoel49/Stashd. Clone it, install Node and the Xcode Command Line Tools if missing, run npm install, then npm run install-local to build it into /Applications and open it.

Updating later

Download Stashd again from this page and drag it into Applications. Click Replace when asked. Your folders and notes are kept.
