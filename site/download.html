Install

Let's get Stashd on your Mac.

The easy way takes about 20 seconds.

The easy way

No warnings · picks your Mac's version

Copy one line, paste it into Terminal, and Stashd installs and opens itself.

curl -fsSL getstashd.vercel.app/install | bash
  1. 1Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return
  2. 2Press ⌘ V, then Return
  3. 3Stashd opens. Look for the little folder in your menu bar

Developer with Node? npx get-stashd does the same.

or download the app

Download Stashd

Free. For Macs with macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. On an Intel Mac? Get the Intel version.

Downloaded the app? Five short steps.

With the easy way, skip to step 4: there's nothing to drag and no "Open Anyway".

  1. 1

    Open the download

    Find Stashd.dmg (or Stashd-Intel.dmg) in your Downloads folder, or click it at the bottom of your browser, and double-click it.

    Drag Stashd into Applications

    A small window opens. Drag the Stashd icon onto the Applications folder. If it asks, click Replace.

  3. 3

    Open it the first time

    Open Stashd from Applications. Your Mac will say it can't check it, because Stashd isn't from the App Store yet. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

    Privacy & Security
    "Stashd" was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
  4. 4

    Follow the welcome

    Stashd opens a short welcome. Click Allow all, then OK in each box your Mac shows: it asks once for each app, like Chrome or Safari, so Stashd can see which tabs and files are open. Apps that aren't open get asked the first time you use them.

    The welcome also asks whether to turn on Stashd AI for smarter notes. For Chrome, click Add to Chrome: a small card appears beside Chrome with the steps, and you drag the helper from the card onto the page. You can skip any step.

    "Stashd" wants to control "Google Chrome".This lets Stashd see which tabs are open, so it can save them.

  5. 5

    Press ⌃S

    That's it. Stashd lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen, as a little folder. Next time you're in the middle of something, press Control-S.

Apple silicon or Intel?

This page picks the right download for your Mac. To check yourself: Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M…, use the main download. If it says Processor: Intel, use the Intel version.

Updating later

Stashd updates itself. When a new version is ready, the menu bar panel shows Restart to update, or it installs the next time Stashd starts. Your folders and notes are kept. (Coming from version 1.4.0 or older? Download it once more from this page and drag it into Applications.)
