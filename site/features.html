Everything Stashd does.

Stashd reads what's open on your Mac and works out which project each tab, file, app and chat belongs to. Everything below comes from that.

Where Stashd's intelligence does the work.
1

Saving your work

One key, and the right things go in the right folder.

  • Press ⌃S

    Stashd reads what you have open and understands what each thing is about. It ticks only what belongs to the project you're on. The basketball game you had on the side stays out.

  • Knows your folders

    Working on something you saved before? Stashd recognises it and adds to that folder instead of making a new one.

  • Learns from you

    Leave something out twice and Stashd stops suggesting it. Add something twice and it's always ticked. Mail, music and messages are never packed.

  • The exact chat

    If you're working with Claude or ChatGPT, the chat you're in is saved with the folder, and Unpack takes you back to that same Claude chat.

  • A note for later

    Type a line when you save, like "book the ryokan this week". It shows up when you come back.

2

Putting it away

Your screen goes clean. Nothing is lost.

  • Pack up

    The folder's windows fly into the menu bar and close. The folder's apps quit like ⌘Q, so anything unsaved still asks first.

  • Tidy all Next update

    One button for the end of the day. It saves the folders you're working in, adds a stray tab to a folder only when it clearly belongs there, and closes everything else.

  • Recently closed Next update

    Everything Tidy all closed stays listed for 10 minutes. Reopen one thing, or all of it.

3

Coming back

Pick up where you left off, not where you think you did.

  • A brief on where you stopped

    Each folder tells you in three short lines what you were last on, what's done and what's next. Written by Apple Intelligence on your Mac, or by Claude if you add your own key.

  • Unpack what you need

    Open everything, or only the files, the tabs or the chats. Untick anything you don't want this time. Something already open never opens twice.

  • Tabs stay together Next update

    A folder's tabs open side by side in their own window, or in a Chrome tab group named after the folder.

  • Pin a folder, press ⌃O Next update

    Pin the project you're on, and ⌃O opens it from anywhere. Nothing pinned? It opens the folder you used last.

  • "You were working on this" Next update

    Open a tab or file from a folder you packed away, and Stashd offers to bring back the rest.

4

Keeping it yours

Your work stays on your Mac.

  • Sorted on your Mac

    The intelligence that sorts your work runs on your Mac. Your folders are saved on your Mac too, not in a cloud.

  • Private windows are skipped

    Incognito and private windows are never saved, and passwords or tokens in web addresses are removed before anything is kept.

  • Free and open source

    Anyone can read the code on GitHub. No account, no subscription.

Try it on your Mac.
