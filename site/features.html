Features

Everything Stashd does, in plain words.

Stashd works out which project each tab, file, app and chat belongs to. Everything below comes from that.

SavingPutting awayComing backBreaksPrivacy

Part 1

Saving your work.

One key, and the right things go in the right folder.

⌃SSmart

Press ⌃S

Stashd reads what you have open and ticks only what belongs to the project you’re on.

▤Smart

Knows your folders

Working on something you saved before? It adds to that folder instead of making a new one.

↻Smart

Learns from you

Leave something out twice and it stops suggesting it. Add something twice and it’s always ticked.

✳

The exact chat

The Claude or ChatGPT chat you’re in is saved too, and Unpack takes you back to that same Claude chat.

✎

A note for later

Type a line when you save, like “book the hotel this week”. It’s there when you come back.

☕Smart

Breaks stay out

A game score or an episode on the side stays out of your work, unless it’s about the project.

Part 2

Putting it away.

Your screen goes clean. Nothing is lost.

⇣

Pack up

The folder’s windows fly into the menu bar and close. Apps quit like ⌘Q, so anything unsaved still asks first.

✦Smart

Tidy all

One button for the end of the day: saves the folders you’re in, files stray tabs where they clearly belong, closes the rest.

⟲

Recently closed

Everything Tidy all closed stays listed for 10 minutes. Reopen one thing, or all of it.

↶

Undo

Changed your mind? Undo is there for 2 seconds after every Pack up.

Part 3

Coming back.

Pick up where you left off, not where you think you did.

☰Smart

A brief on where you stopped

Three short lines: what you were last on, what’s done, what’s next.

▶

Unpack what you need

Open everything, or only the files, tabs or chats. Something already open never opens twice.

◉

Open one thing

Click any tab or file inside a folder to open just that one.

▦

Chrome tab groups

A folder’s Chrome tabs open together in a tab group named after the folder.

⌃O

Pin a folder, press ⌃O

Pin the project you’re on and ⌃O opens it from anywhere.

✋Smart

“You were working on this”

Open a tab or file from a packed folder, and Stashd offers to bring back the rest.

⇄Smart

Merge look-alikes

Two folders that are really the same work? Stashd spots them and offers to merge.

✕

Fix what’s inside

Remove anything from a folder with one click, with Undo.

Part 4

Breaks, kept apart.

A Work / Break switch in the menu bar. Your fun stays out of your work.

★

Your places

YouTube, Netflix, Spotify, or any site you add, one click away. Rename or remove them anytime.

▶

Continue watching

Videos you close land here, with a thumbnail and how much is left.

●

Live streams

Twitch, Kick and live YouTube streams get a red Live tag.

✂Smart

No Shorts

Shorts, Reels and TikToks never clutter the list. Only things worth coming back to.

☕Smart

“Taking a break?”

Press ⌃S with only videos open, and they go to Continue watching instead of a work folder.

⌛

Fades on its own

One-off clips leave after a week. Shows and things you reopen stay a month.

Part 5

Keeping it yours.

Your work stays on your Mac.

⌂

Sorted on your Mac

Your folders are saved on your Mac, not in a cloud. No account.

◐

Private windows skipped

Incognito and private windows are never saved. Passwords and tokens in web addresses are removed.

⌥

Your own Claude key

Briefs use Claude only if you add your own key, or Apple Intelligence on your Mac.
