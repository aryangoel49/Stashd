Just three steps.
Two of them are one key.

You never make a folder or drag anything into one. Stashd reads what's open and sorts it for you, right on your Mac.

1

Stash

Press ⌃S. Stashd ticks only what belongs to the project you're on. Add a note for later if you like.

Pack up

The windows fly into the menu bar and the folder goes to sleep. Unsaved work still asks to be saved first.

3

Unpack

Open the folder when you're back. A short brief says where you stopped, and everything comes back.

It knows what goes together.

You're planning a trip to Japan, with a basketball game and your inbox open on the side. Press ⌃S and Stashd picks out the flights, the hotel, the train pass and your plan, because it understands what each one is about. The basketball game and your inbox stay open.

It learns as you go: leave something out twice and it remembers. All of this runs on your Mac.
