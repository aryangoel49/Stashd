Three steps, and two of them are one key.

Stashd lives in your menu bar. It doesn't change how you work. It just remembers it for you.

controlS
Step 1

Stash

Press ⌃S. Stashd ticks only what belongs to the project you're on. Add a note for later if you like.

GBW
Step 2

Pack up

The windows fly into the menu bar and the folder goes to sleep. Unsaved work still asks to be saved first.

Japan trip
Step 3

Unpack

Open the folder when you're back. A short brief says where you stopped, and everything comes back.

It takes the project, not every tab.

Stashd reads what your tabs are about, so it knows that a lecture PDF and a Krebs cycle video go together, even with no words in common. Instagram stays out.

Sorting runs on your Mac, in an instant.
