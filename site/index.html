Good questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Stashd is free and open source.

Which apps does it work with?

Tabs in Chrome, Safari and Arc. Files in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Numbers, Keynote, Preview and TextEdit. It reopens the exact Claude chat you were in, and remembers your other apps too.

Does it close my work without asking?

No. Save keeps everything open. Pack up closes it, with Undo for 2 seconds, and anything unsaved still asks you first.

What writes the note on where I stopped?

Claude, if you add your own key (about $0.0003 a note), or Apple Intelligence on your Mac if it's turned on. Otherwise a simple built-in note. It only sees names, titles and addresses, with secrets removed.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 12 or newer on Apple silicon (M1 or later). On an Intel Mac you can build it yourself from GitHub.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Stashd isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. The install guide shows the one-time step.
