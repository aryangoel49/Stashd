## How it works

**1. Stash it**
Press ⌃S. Stashd ticks the tabs, files and apps that belong to this job. Leave yourself a note if you like ("halfway through paragraph 3"), then press Enter.

**2. Let it sleep**
Tick "Tidy up" and those windows fly into the menu bar. Spotify and Messages stay where they are.

**3. Wake it up**
Press ⌃W and pick the job. Read the brief, then click "Pick up where I left off". Your essay, and the two tabs you hadn't read yet, come back to the front.

---

## Privacy

Your jobs stay on your Mac, and Stashd sorts them there too. It goes online only to write a Claude brief, and only if you connect your own key. Even then, Claude sees file names and tab titles. It never sees what's inside your files.
